RULES REGARDING SOUNDS

All sounds must be labeled SFX or Song depending on their use and their name.
example: SFX-Click (this is a click sound used for SFX), Song-Lofi(this is a Lofi song )

SFX (SOUND EFFECTS) need to be under 1 second and have to be in .WAV
Songs need to be under 20 seconds and the format .OGG or .MP3

See the examples on the Songs folder
